# Art asset checklist
- NXP operative: rigged humanoid, idle/run/strafe/fire/reload/hit/death/dash.
- Enemy soldier: rigged humanoid + same core combat clips.
- Spider drone: locomotion/fire/hit/death.
- Mutant Brute: idle/run/slam/charge/hit/death.
- Weapons: rifle, SMG, energy rifle; muzzle sockets.
- Neon city modular kit: road, sidewalk, facades, storefronts, rooftop pieces.
- Props: cars, barriers, crates, dumpsters, lamps, holographic signs.
- Materials: wet asphalt, concrete, painted metal, glass, emissive neon.
- VFX: muzzle, tracer, impacts, explosion, smoke, sparks, core, boss slam.
All production assets should be licensed for commercial use and their licenses recorded.
